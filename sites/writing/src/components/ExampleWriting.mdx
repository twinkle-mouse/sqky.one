When the sun rises, she’s already up, preening and
fretting over her fur with a collection of combs.

It was already very neat,
but that wasn’t what she worried about.

It already looked adorable, even, to most of her peers…
but that wasn’t what she worried about.

By the time her morning alarm rings, it looked good enough to
her eyes, so she finally moved on with the day.
