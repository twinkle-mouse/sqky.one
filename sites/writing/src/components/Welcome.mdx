## Welcome!

In this glimmering library, we tell the stories of the stars in your heart,
we sing the lyrics of the twinkles in your eyes,
and we hurt, we cry, we live, we love—and we hope to make you feel a little less lonely.

Most works here are created by *Stella Sparkles*, though you may occasionally see others on here.
If you have any thoughts, please [reach out](https://sqky.one/#contacts)!
We’re always *ecstatic* to hear your thoughts.
<small>(P.S. If you have any suggestions for the content warnings, please *please* reach out.)</small>
